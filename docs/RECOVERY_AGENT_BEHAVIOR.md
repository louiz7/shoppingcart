# Recovery conversation and tool boundaries

GPT Live and its existing reasoning model interpret conversation intent. There are no runtime keyword lists, fixed acceptance phrases, or additional consent-classifier model calls. The voice prompt provides flexible guidance for objections, listening, interruptions and delegation. The founder persona remains disclosed as AI working for the founding team.

The reasoning agent selects `confirm_contact`, `send_checkout_link(use_discount=...)` and `stop_contact`. A scoped refusal (for example, declining a discount) does not automatically stop all contact. An actual contact stop must invoke `stop_contact` immediately. Discount creation alone never means acceptance; sending defaults to a plain link with the discount parameter removed.

The worker serializes committed LiveKit conversation messages and binds gated tool requests to the current user event. Event IDs are injected by the worker, never supplied by the model. The service checks call/shop ownership, recency, latest-user-event identity, active contact permission, current cart and approved offer, and rechecks authorization before provider dispatch. A discounted send also requires a non-interrupted assistant message after offer creation and before the user's response. The model interprets what those messages mean; these structural checks do not independently prove semantic agreement or that audio reached the listener.

Missing transcript evidence, interrupted responses and changes during a pending action fail closed. Stop and hangup remain available even when transcript persistence fails. Transcript ingestion itself does not classify intent. Unknown provider outcomes remain durable and are not retried. Only delivery receipts establish SMS delivery, and only verified Shopify orders establish purchases.

Product answers use freshly loaded Shopify cart/product data. Empty descriptions and unavailable shipping/return facts remain unknown; the agent may refer to the verified shop support address. There is no invented FAQ or claim of historical objection-handling success. Shopify legal-policy ingestion would require the additional `read_legal_policies` permission and is not enabled by this change.

## Verification

- Backend regressions: `RECOVERY_DB_TEST=1 node --test test/recovery-{policy,integration,sms-flow,discounts}.test.js` against the isolated local database.
- Offline SDK contracts: recovery virtualenv `python -m unittest discover -s agent -p test_recovery.py`.
- Opt-in semantic evaluation: recovery virtualenv `python agent/evaluate_recovery.py`. Uses synthetic DE/EN dialogs, the deployed reasoning instructions and SDK-generated tool schemas. Calls OpenAI only; never executes business tools, dials or sends SMS. Model usage is billable. Not a voice/acoustic or complete E2E test.

API and worker must be released together: older workers do not send `userEventId` and fail closed on contact confirmation/SMS. Production releases use GitHub Actions. A telephone acceptance test verifies audible delivery, interruptions, live tool round trips, SMS receipt and actual checkout discount behavior.

## Alternative products and prepared checkout links

`search_products` reads a bounded selection from the connected shop, optionally below a maximum unit price. Results contain real descriptions, variants and current prices, not inferred quality or appearance claims. Publication is verified using Shopify's `publishedAt`; `onlineStoreUrl` was null even for published test-shop products. Unpublished, sold-out, subscription-only, gift-card and component-dependent variants are excluded. Search examines up to eight products and ten variants each, returns at most six choices, and explicitly reports that it is not exhaustive. More specific search terms can refine results.

`prepare_checkout` previews the complete requested selection (up to ten distinct variants, quantity 1–10 each). It never edits the abandoned checkout, purchases anything or sends a message. It revalidates variants in this shop, calculates the subtotal, and optionally verifies a merchant-approved discount against the new subtotal. Proposals belong to the current call, expire for sending after 30 minutes, and corrections supersede older proposals. Repeating an unchanged proposal preserves its review.

After presenting the proposal and receiving natural agreement plus an SMS request, the agent uses `send_checkout_link(prepared_checkout_id=..., use_discount=...)`. The existing worker-bound conversation evidence and backend contact controls apply. An uninterrupted assistant message must follow preparation and precede the current user response. The model interprets agreement; no phrase matcher was added. Selected prices and availability are checked again before reserving and dispatching the SMS. Changes require a new preview. A discount is only included with `use_discount=true`, and must be verified for this specific proposal. A previously applied discount in the abandoned cart is not copied.

The link uses Shopify's supported `/cart/variant:quantity` permalink with a random proposal identifier in cart attributes. Legacy cart permalinks are used because the newer `/buy` format does not yet support cart attributes needed for attribution. Customer email/address/phone are not embedded in the URL. Shopify collects or autofills buyer details, validates final pricing, shipping and taxes, and creates the actual checkout when the link opens. No order or payment is created by preparing the link. Store password protection still applies.

SMS deduplication is per verified target, with a maximum of three distinct messages per abandoned checkout. This permits an explicitly requested alternative after an original link was already sent, while identical targets and unknown dispatch outcomes are never resent automatically. Signed Shopify order webhooks associate a new checkout back to recovery only when the random proposal, same shop, recipient phone, selected variants, sent SMS and seven-day call window match. Existing paid/test/refund exclusions still govern reported revenue. This is linked-sale attribution, not a claim of incremental revenue.

Verification: local PostgreSQL tests cover preview → review → SMS → paid-order attribution, concurrent duplicate requests, changed selections/prices/inventory, contact stops, cross-tenant IDs, correction during dispatch, and ambiguous provider outcomes. Nineteen offline SDK checks and 24 real reasoning-model cases passed (`evaluate_recovery.py` plus `evaluate_catalog.py`); the latter use synthetic tools with no business effects. A real browser opened the alternative Hydrogen snowboard checkout at USD 600 with neither the Liquid item nor the old discount; no payment was submitted. The subsequent owner telephone test completed: the agent found the Hydrogen snowboard, prepared it at USD 600, obtained agreement to the presented selection and sent one SMS with a signed delivered receipt. Opening that exact SMS link after the visitor password showed Hydrogen, no Liquid item and no discount. No payment was submitted. The call ended and temporary test services/settings were cleaned up.

## Quality objections and duplicate lookups, 2026-11-02

In the latest owner call, the quality/price objection was followed by `confirm_contact` with its combined context read (729 ms), then an unnecessary second context read (521 ms). The next speech-start sample after that second result was 4,768 ms. The Shopify reads together took 1,250 ms; they do not explain the whole delay. Transcript timestamps denote committed messages, not the beginning of each spoken answer. Existing telemetry cannot split the remaining delay precisely into backend reasoning, delegation and voice scheduling.

The prompt now treats a broad quality concern as a conversational sales objection: respond positively and directly, use a relevant verified benefit when available, and clarify the specific concern only when unclear. Quality is expressed as a shop aspiration (for example, “Gute Qualität ist uns wichtig”), not evidence of an unverified material, certification, warranty or lifespan. Unrequested disclaimers about absent certifications are discouraged. A specific unanswered factual question still receives a narrow, honest explanation. Mixed price/quality concerns should not reflexively trigger a discount.

Both delegation guidance and the context tool description now distinguish an unread fact from a known missing field. A successful combined contact/context result does not need another cart read; an empty description is not a reason to repeat the same query. This is model guidance, not a deterministic prohibition or cache. Actual checkout/discount/SMS actions retain their existing fresh backend checks, and the agent can still refresh when the customer reports a change.

Research applied: [OpenAI GPT-Live prompting](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/live-prompting) recommends a compact conversational prompt, separate backend procedures and direct answers from current context. [Vapi's prompting guide](https://docs.vapi.ai/prompting-guide) contributes concise role/style instructions and short examples; its [sales assistant template](https://docs.vapi.ai/assistants/examples/lead-qualification) contributes a focused sales objective and tools only when needed. We did not import rigid BANT qualification, fixed word limits or forced filler into cart recovery. The existing Handle Objection skill informs responding to the underlying concern with supported proof points.

Verification adds six synthetic real-model cases: general quality in DE/EN, mixed price/quality, a requested but unsupported ten-year guarantee, empty product descriptions and quality questions after combined contact/context loading. All 22 recovery cases and eight alternative-cart cases passed on the final prompt, alongside 19 offline SDK checks. Automated checks assert no unnecessary tool invocation; printed synthetic responses were reviewed manually for tone and unsupported claims. These checks exercise the reasoning backend, not GPT-Live audio or its delegation decisions. A new telephone test is required to measure the resulting audible latency; no latency improvement is claimed from the prompt change alone. Review of the changed prompts preserves AI disclosure, tenant-bound tools, untrusted product-data treatment and all action authorization. No dedicated Python/LiveKit security reference is available in the installed security skill.


## Natural objection handling follow-up

The subsequent owner call lasted 3:27. General quality reassurance used no product lookup; later specific questions used one cart/context read. The agent prepared Hydrogen at USD 540 after the accepted 10% offer, and Twilio confirmed the SMS delivered. Room deletion and restoration of the temporary test settings were confirmed. This call preceded the following wording refinement; the new discounted checkout was not independently opened in that run.

Research: [Gong's objection handling guide](https://www.gong.io/blog/objection-handling-techniques) emphasizes identifying the actual concern, relevant reframing and conversational turn-taking. [Salesforce Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/objection-handling-strategies/learn-how-to-handle-common-objections) teaches acknowledging, discovering and responding. [HubSpot's objection handling guide](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/handling-common-sales-objections) emphasizes curiosity, tailored evidence and avoiding defensive arguments or reflexive discounts. These are largely B2B training resources; their principles inform our conversational design, not a claim of measured conversion lift for this shop.

Applied as flexible guidance, not a fixed sequence: connect a brief honest limitation naturally to a relevant verified benefit or the quality stance explicitly requested by the project owner. The owner's preferred example is “Das genaue Material kann ich dir gerade nicht sagen, aber ich kann dir versichern, dass wir extrem auf die Qualität unserer Produkte achten.” This conveys the shop's quality emphasis, not a certification, product guarantee or claim that a particular material was verified. Avoid appended slogans, repeated reassurance and automatic support referrals. An ordinary material question alone does not require a handoff. Explicit purchase conditions and safety questions remain unresolved without facts; a relevant support route can then be offered. No follow-up, research or transfer is promised without the capability to execute it.

The test suite adds DE/EN missing-material questions, repeated detail questions, an allergy-dependent purchase condition, thinking time without SMS and a cheaper competitor. Empty-material fixtures remove material clues from the product title as well as the description. Tone and factuality are reviewed from printed synthetic replies; automated checks cover tool decisions, including no unwanted send or contact stop. Logging a non-refusal outcome during thinking time is allowed. A legitimate price objection may result in checking an approved offer; this is separate from accepting or sending it. One intermediate test incorrectly included a prepared but unaccepted discount in an SMS request; the send tool description was clarified at the decision point and the acceptance regression suite rerun. No keyword consent matcher or new runtime authorization was added.

Final verification: 28 recovery-model cases and eight catalog-model cases passed; 19 offline SDK checks passed. Manual review found no invented material or allergy assurance in the final samples. Wording is probabilistic: the reasoning model sometimes still repeats a general quality phrase on a follow-up, so natural delivery must be assessed in a fresh voice call. Review/security found no new action or data boundary changes; the installed security skill has no dedicated Python/LiveKit reference.
